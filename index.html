About Global College

Our Journey of Excellence and Nation Building

History & Evolution

Global College was established in 2009 by Alhaji Abdulrahman Babatunde Elesin. The institution humbly began its journey as a Nursery and Primary school located in the Olohunlanbe Community, Ajijola Area, Ede, Osun State, gaining full accreditation from the Osun State Government.

Following years of academic excellence, the school expanded to include Junior Secondary education. To accommodate our growing facilities, the secondary section relocated to its permanent site at Ifedayo, Olomo-Oba Community, Awere Musa Area, Ede, Osun State, where it now runs smoothly as a standard and fully accredited secondary institution.

General Entry Requirements (Nigerian Educational Guidelines)

Crèche & Nursery

Birth Certificate, Immunization Card, and completed application form.

Primary (1–5)

Application form, birth certificate, passport photographs, and previous school report cards.

Junior Secondary (JSS 1–3)

Primary School Leaving Testimonial, past academic reports, and entrance placement test.

Senior Secondary (SSS 1–3)

BECE / JSCE results, JSS 3 transfer clearance, and qualifying stream assessment.
